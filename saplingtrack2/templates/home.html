How it works

1

Register

On planting day, the organiser records each sapling with its GPS location, species and one named volunteer. It takes under a minute.

2

Care

Each volunteer gets a personal link on WhatsApp. No account needed. They log a watering or report damage in three taps.

3

Track

The dashboard shows the real survival rate, with overdue and damaged saplings at the top so the organiser knows what needs help.

Why it matters

Unmonitored drives often see most of their saplings die within two years, wasting the trees, the water and the volunteers' time. Giving every sapling one responsible person turns a single planting morning into two years of care.
